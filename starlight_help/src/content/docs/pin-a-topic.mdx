Pinning a topic keeps an important topic easy to find in an active channel.
Channel administrators can pin one topic in each channel.

Pinning a topic:

* Puts a 📌 at the beginning of the topic name, e.g., `example topic`
  becomes `📌 example topic`.
* Lists the topic first in the channel's topic list in the left sidebar, with a
  pin icon in place of the 📌.
* Unpins the channel's current pinned topic, if there is one.
* Triggers an automated notice from Notification Bot indicating that the
  topic was pinned.

Unpinning a topic removes the 📌 and also triggers a notice from
Notification Bot.

Pinned topics have these restrictions:

* Only channel administrators can pin, unpin, rename, or start a pinned topic.
* Messages can't be moved into or out of a pinned topic.
* A topic can't be both pinned and resolved.

<ZulipNote>
  The mobile app shows the 📌 in a pinned topic's name, but can't pin or
  unpin a topic.
</ZulipNote>

## Pin a topic

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <TopicActions />

      1. Select **Pin topic**.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    <ZulipTip>
      You can also click the **pin** (<PinIcon />) icon in the message
      recipient bar to pin an unpinned topic, or rename the topic to start
      with 📌 followed by a space.
    </ZulipTip>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Configure who can post in a pinned topic

<AdminOnly />

By default, anyone who can send messages in a channel can post in its pinned
topic. You can restrict this to a group of users. The same permission is
needed to edit a message in the pinned topic. Pinning and unpinning a topic
works regardless of this setting.

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="relative/channel/all" />

  1. Select a channel.

  <SelectChannelViewPermissions />

  1. Under **Moderation permissions**, configure **Who can post in this
     channel's pinned topic**.

  <SaveChanges />
</FlattenedSteps>
